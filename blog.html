About

Nick Vujicic (born December 4, 1982) is an Australian-American motivational speaker, evangelist, and author. He was born with tetra-amelia syndrome, a rare disorder characterized by the absence of all four limbs. Despite his physical challenges, Nick has overcome incredible odds to inspire millions of people around the world through his story of faith, perseverance, and positivity.

Career

Nick began speaking publicly at the age of 19, sharing his life story to encourage people facing difficulties. He founded "Life Without Limbs" in 2005, a non-profit organization dedicated to spreading hope and inspiration globally. Over the years, he has spoken in more than 70 countries, reaching millions of people in schools, prisons, churches, stadiums, and through digital platforms. He is also an accomplished author with bestsellers like “Life Without Limits,” “Unstoppable,” “Stand Strong,” and “Love Without Limits.”

Educational Life

Nick completed his schooling in Brisbane, Australia. Despite being bullied during his early school years, he became a head boy and eventually earned a Bachelor's degree in Commerce, with a double major in Accounting and Financial Planning from Griffith University.

Family

Nick's parents, Boris and Dushka Vujicic, are Serbian immigrants to Australia. His father worked as a computer programmer and his mother was a nurse. They raised Nick with strong Christian values, encouraging him to face the world with courage. Nick has a brother and a sister who supported him throughout his journey.

Marriage Life

In 2012, Nick married Kanae Miyahara. Their story of love and faith captured the hearts of people worldwide. Together, they have four children. Kanae plays a supportive role in his mission, often appearing alongside him in interviews and talks.
